
# Retail Business Intelligence & Analytics

### SQL-Based Retail Business Performance Analysis

## 1. Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing the business performance of FreshMart, a fictional supermarket chain operating across multiple branches in India. Using SQL, the project examines retail data to identify business trends, evaluate operational performance, and generate meaningful insights.

## 2. Project Objectives

- Analyze revenue and profitability across branches.
- Evaluate employee sales performance.
- Identify the most profitable products and categories.
- Understand customer purchasing behaviour.
- Examine inventory levels and stock availability.
- Generate data-driven recommendations for business improvement.

## 3. Dataset Description

The project uses a relational retail database consisting of the following tables:

- Branches
- Employees
- Customers
- Products
- Suppliers
- Orders
- Order Details
- Inventory

## 4. Tools & Technologies

- SQL – Data querying and analysis
- Relational Database – Retail data management
- Microsoft Word – Project documentation

## 5. Analysis Performed

- Branch-wise revenue and profit analysis
- Employee sales performance analysis
- Product and category profitability analysis
- Customer purchasing pattern analysis
- Inventory and stock availability analysis

## 6. Key Findings

- Pune recorded the highest total revenue and profit.
- Bengaluru achieved a higher profit margin.
- Jithin Jose from the Thrissur branch was the top-performing employee.
- Electronics was the most profitable product category.
- Potato Chips 100g was the top-selling product by quantity.
- Zero-stock inventory records were identified across multiple branches.

## 7. Business Recommendations

- Evaluate high-performing branches for potential future expansion.
- Monitor branch-level profitability and performance.
- Recognize and study the practices of high-performing employees.
- Focus on profitable product categories.
- Improve inventory monitoring to reduce stock availability issues.
